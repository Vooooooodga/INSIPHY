# IntraPhy roadmap after 0.19.1

Purpose remains genomic FASTA/GFF/tree to **exon structural evolution only**.

Implemented audit corrections: atomic annotation alternatives, full available
locus search, physical-region uniqueness, canonical branches with explicit origin
sensitivity, complete-state preflight and bounded numerical caching, declared
multi-exon insertion payloads, weighted gene bootstrap, nested-optimization checks,
and current model-linked explanatory and separate CTMC result diagrams.

Scientific acceptance remains: independently validated MAMSTR/FDPS and deletion
cases, comparative correspondence benchmarking, discovery-aware statistical
calibration, parameter/annotation/alignment sensitivity and empirical coverage.
These tasks must not be marked complete because synthetic tests pass.

Not part of this release: exon usage, PSI, transcriptome inputs, selection or
repair-enzyme inference, full ancestral transcript repertoires, arbitrary exon
copy genealogies, inversions and shuffling, or unbounded extinct-ancestor states.
